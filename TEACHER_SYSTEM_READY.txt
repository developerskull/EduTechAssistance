==============================================================
💡 HOW IT WORKS
==============================================================

WORKFLOW:
1. Teacher creates assignment with questions
2. Teacher assigns to students
3. Students receive assignments
4. Students take exams
5. Multiple choice & true/false auto-graded
6. Teacher evaluates short answers
7. Students see final scores & feedback

AUTO-GRADING:
- Multiple choice questions are graded instantly
- True/false questions are graded instantly
- Short answer questions need teacher review
